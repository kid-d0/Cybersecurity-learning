Introduction to Cybersecurity 🔐
 1.What is Cybersecurity
   -Cybersecurity is the practise of protecting computers, networks, systems and data from unauthorized access, attacks and damage
 2.The CIA Triad
   -It consists of three important principles:
   >Confidentiality: Ensuring information is only enclosed to authorised access
   >Intregity: Ensuring information stays accurate and is not modified by unauthorized access
   >Availability: Ensuring systems and information is accessible when needed
3.Why Cybersecurity Matters
   -Protects personal and organizational information
   -Prevents unauthorized access to systems
   -Helps reduce the risk of cyberattacks
   -Protects the privacy of the users
